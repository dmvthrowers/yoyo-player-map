# Venue & Insurance Research Skill

## Purpose
Automate venue option extraction and insurance research for event planning.

## Features
- Extract venue options from emails to Excel/Sheets
- Research and summarize insurance options for COI
- Track status of venue and insurance requirements

## Usage
- Input: Email text, venue/insurance requirements
- Output: Excel/Sheets, summary reports

## Integration
- Google Sheets, Outlook, Gmail
