# Social & Email Management Skill

## Purpose
Automate social media scheduling, posting, and email response management.

## Features
- Schedule and post to social media platforms
- Draft and send email responses
- Manage mailing lists and campaigns

## Usage
- Input: Post content, email drafts, schedule
- Output: Social posts, sent emails, campaign reports

## Integration
- Instagram, Facebook, MailerLite, Gmail
